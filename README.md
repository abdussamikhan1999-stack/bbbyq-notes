# BBBYQ Notes

Notes distilled from a `/BBBYQ/` (Bed Bath & Beyond post-bankruptcy stock)
thread. **This one needs a much stronger factual warning than the other
threads in this series** — unlike GME (a real, still-operating,
exchange-listed company where the community theory is speculative but
layered on top of a real company), Bed Bath & Beyond's equity has a
**documented, court-confirmed outcome that directly contradicts this
thread's core premise.**

See [LINKS.md](LINKS.md) for the full raw link list. Informational only,
not financial advice — and in this specific case, the documented facts
below indicate real, substantial financial risk in treating this thread's
premise as investable.

## The documented, confirmed fact (not in dispute)

Bed Bath & Beyond Inc.'s Chapter 11 bankruptcy plan was **confirmed by the
bankruptcy court on September 29, 2023**. Per that confirmed plan:

> "Each allowed interest in BBB shall be canceled, released, and
> extinguished, and no holder of interests in BBB shall be entitled to any
> recovery or distribution under the Plan."

In plain terms: **existing BBBY common stock was formally cancelled with
$0 recovery for shareholders.** Unsecured creditors recovered only ~2.5%
of their $2.4B in claims, funded by liquidation sales, lease sales, the
Bed Bath & Beyond brand name sale to Overstock, and the Buy Buy Baby IP
sale to Dream on Me. This is reported consistently across CNBC, Seeking
Alpha, InvestorPlace, and the SEC's own filings (see Sources below) — it
is not one outlet's interpretation, it's the confirmed plan's own text.

**"BBBYQ" shares that continue to trade OTC after this date represent
equity that the bankruptcy court has already ruled is worthless.** Shares
of bankrupt companies often keep trading on over-the-counter markets on
pure speculation even after a confirmed plan cancels them — this is a
well-documented pattern with defunct/"zombie" tickers generally, not
unique to BBBY, and it's specifically what CNBC's contemporaneous coverage
warned retail holders about.

## The community's counter-theory (what this thread actually argues)

This thread's premise is that the bankruptcy was a **deliberate corporate
restructuring, not a real failure** — that the surviving shell entity
(renamed "DK-Butterfly-1 Inc.") retained large tax-loss carryforwards
(NOLs), and that GameStop will eventually acquire this shell in a reverse
merger, which the theory claims would (a) hand GameStop a large tax shield,
(b) legally require former BBBY shareholders to receive GameStop shares
under Section 382(l)(5) tax rules to preserve those NOLs, and (c) thereby
trigger a massive short squeeze as "phantom shares" (from the OTC ticker
still trading post-cancellation) become real delivery obligations. Ryan
Cohen's board involvement, Carl Icahn's prior restructuring track record,
and the timing of GameStop's own warrant dividends are all cited as
supporting circumstantial evidence.

**This theory does not explain away the confirmed cancellation above** — it
argues the cancellation is somehow not final or will be worked around via
a future corporate action. That is speculative in a way that a
court-confirmed, already-executed Chapter 11 plan does not typically get
reversed by. The thesis document itself (bbbwhy.com) is unusually candid
about this, explicitly stating "where the thesis speculates, it says so" —
worth taking that self-disclosure seriously rather than skipping past it,
and worth noting that its central financial figure (a "$3.5 billion NOL"
claim) is described in the source itself as "community analysis," not an
independently verified, audited number.

## What's worth actually checking, if you want ground truth

- **Kroll Restructuring Administration's BBBY case page**
  (`restructuring.ra.kroll.com/bbby/`) — the official claims-agent site for
  this specific bankruptcy case, with the actual docket and filed plan
  documents. This is the primary source, not a summary of it.
- The **September 2023 8-K** filed by DK-Butterfly-1, Inc. with the SEC
  (linked in Sources below) is the actual notice of the confirmed plan.
- **jake2b's YouTube channel** and **bbbwhy.com** are community
  theory-promotion content, not independent verification — treat them the
  same way the GME thread's own disclaimer treats Reddit DDs: not fact.
